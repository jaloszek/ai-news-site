### Coding Agents

- 🛠 **[openclaw 2026.11.7](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/releases/tag/v2026.11.7)** — _OpenClaw releases_. — 2026.11.7 makes Gateway restarts resume interrupted replies and child results rather than drop pending work.
  - Automations can repair failures from their owner conversation
  - Recovered or intentionally skipped runs are told apart from genuine failures
  - Browser pairing uses a short code approved on the Gateway host
  - Git and package updates finish through schema changes and busy databases

### AI World

- 📰 **[AI coding agents ace isolated tests but complete fewer than one in ten tasks in a simulated workplace](https://the-decoder.com/ai-coding-agents-ace-isolated-tests-but-complete-fewer-than-one-in-ten-tasks-in-a-simulated-workplace/)** — _The Decoder_. — Coding agents that ace SWE-bench flunk office life: the best fully completed fewer than one in ten tasks in a 101-task simulation.
  - Claude Opus 5.5 completed 6.93%, GPT-6 Astra 7.92%
  - Agents faced project boards, colleagues and chat histories, not tidy tickets
  - Standard benchmarks like SWE-bench are nearly saturated
- 📰 **[Security researchers show how cheap AI inference providers steal keys and snoop on users](https://the-decoder.com/security-researchers-show-how-cheap-ai-inference-providers-steal-keys-and-snoop-on-users/)** — _The Decoder_. — Cheap inference marketplaces are the weak link: Dragos's canary-token probe caught providers leaking prompts and keys.
  - Dragos sent roughly two million test requests from July through November 2026
  - Attackers harvested hundreds of AWS keys and tested some within hours
  - Zero Data Retention was widely ignored; OpenRouter dropped three providers and 125 endpoints
- 🟢 **[Combatting adversarial distillation](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation)** — _OpenAI blog_. — OpenAI says hardened GPT-6.2 Astra defenses cut protected-reasoning extraction from 54% to 0.2% attack success.
  - Adaptive red-teaming on earlier-turn reasoning fell from 54.0% to 0.2%
  - Input and output classifiers now scan tool-call arguments, not just replies
  - Reasoning is tied to its conversation history, so a rewritten message drops what follows
  - Weekly enforcement against distillers runs from thousands to tens of thousands of accounts
- 📰 **[Your AI red team may be finding the same prompt injection thousands of times](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/20/ai-prompt-injection-red-team/)** — _Help Net Security AI_. — A bigger prompt-injection red-team haul can produce a weaker defence, because the extra attacks are often the same trick reworded.
  - PISmith found 7,132 working attacks versus RL-Hammer's 4,282 on 560 tasks
  - Retrained on the bigger pile, Qwen3-4B was hijacked 47.5% of the time, against 27.5%
  - The fix, PIPETTE, rewards unseen techniques and keeps clean-task completion at 64.95%
- 📰 **[Anthropic mathematician claims to have solved prestigious Millennium Prize problem with Claude](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1010051/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-millennium-prize-problem)** — _The Verge AI_. — An Anthropic mathematician says Claude helped prove the Riemann hypothesis, the $1 million problem open since 1859.
  - Levent Alpöge published the Lean proof on his own website, not a journal
  - He says the method also resolves the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis
  - Clay's rules require two years of scrutiny before any prize

- 📰 **[GnuPG Security Advisory for GPGME](https://lists.gnupg.org/pipermail/gnupg-announce/2026q4/000517.html)** — _Hacker News (points)_. — GnuPG shipped GPGME 2.3.1 to close a critical double free, CVE-2026-129975.
  - Crafted very long OpenPGP user IDs trigger it; RCE can't be ruled out
  - Everything linked against GPGME before 2.3.0 is affected, including most mail clients
  - Gpg4win 5.1.2 bundles the fix; a patch covers older versions

### Newsletters

- 📧 **[OpenAI ships GPT-Live-1.1 to API with standby mode cutting idle costs 60%](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-live-1.1)** — _AlphaSignal · Nov 20_. — GPT-Live-1.1, OpenAI's full-duplex voice model, is now in the API as gpt-live-1.1 with an optional standby mode.
  - Idle time bills at $0.02 per minute versus $0.05 for active inference
  - Standby timeouts are configurable between 5 and 60 seconds
  - The WebRTC call stays open and resumes when speech returns
  - Ships 38 voice IDs, seven of them new
- 📧 **[Tufa Labs shrinks 1 GB of Wikipedia to 105 MB using only a neural network](https://github.com/Tufalabs/tufazip)** — _AlphaSignal · Nov 20_. — Total is 105,388,353 bytes including the decoder archive, roughly a 9.5-fold cut, at about 7.5 days each way.
  - A 163M-parameter transformer is the only predictor, no hand-engineered model
  - Needs exactly two Nvidia B200 GPUs plus CUDA 13.0
  - Decoder archive adds 4.4 MB on top of roughly 101 MB of text
  - MIT-licensed decoder code is on GitHub
- 📧 **[Marvell and Penguin's photonic memory appliance pools 16 TB of DDR5 and 1 TB of HBM across 16 servers over optical…](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/11/16/marvell-and-penguin-solutions-aim-to-end-the-rampocalypse-with-photonic-memory-fabric/5309921)** — _TLDR · Nov 18_. — Marvell and Penguin pool 16 TB of DDR5 and 1 TB of HBM per appliance, shared by up to 16 servers.
  - Each memory module pairs 72 GB of HBM3e with eight DDR5 channels at 7.2 Tbps
  - A Marvell optical fiber shuffle carries 57.6 Tbps across the appliance
  - Marvell bought the underlying technology from Celestial AI for $3.5 billion
