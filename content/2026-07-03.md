# Friday, July 3, 2026

### Coding Agents

- 🛠 **[v2.1.199](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.199)** — _Claude Code releases_. — Stacked slash-skills (`/skill-a /skill-b`) now load all leading skills up to 5, SSL handshake failures skip retries and surface the fix hint immediately, and partial streaming output survives mid-stream API errors instead of being discarded.
- 🛠 **[JetBrains 7.0.1-rc.14](https://github.com/Kilo-Org/kilocode/releases/tag/jetbrains%2Fv7.0.1-rc.14)** — _Kilo Code releases_. — Adds Agent Behavior settings and a richer model picker with routed model info and clearer badges, plus more reliable backend startup recovery when event streams stall or reconnect.
- 🛠 **[v1.41.0](https://github.com/aaif-goose/goose/releases/tag/v1.41.0)** — _Goose releases_. — Adds iFlytek Spark and Astron MaaS providers, an `--edit` flag for pre-fork conversation editing, GLM-5.2 model support, global hints from `~/.agents/AGENTS.md`, and desktop localization for 8 new languages.
- 🛠 **[v1.40.0](https://github.com/aaif-goose/goose/releases/tag/v1.40.0)** — _Goose releases_. — Adds `/status`, `/model`, and `/goal` slash commands for session insights, model switching, and self-evaluation, plus `goose review` for local code review and a hooks system for custom extensibility.

### AI World

- 📰 **[Microsoft launches $2.5 billion "Frontier Company" to embed 6,000 AI engineers inside enterprise clients](https://the-decoder.com/microsoft-launches-2-5-billion-frontier-company-to-embed-6000-ai-engineers-inside-enterprise-clients/)** — _The Decoder_. — Microsoft is putting $2.5 billion behind Frontier Company, embedding 6,000 engineers directly with enterprise clients — positioning itself as a platform-neutral alternative to the dedicated deployment arms OpenAI and Anthropic have each set up.
- 📰 **[Anthropic says it cut 80 percent of Claude Code's system prompt because Fable 5 models "want a smaller system prompt"](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-says-it-cut-80-percent-of-claude-codes-system-prompt-because-fable-5-models-want-a-smaller-system-prompt/)** — _The Decoder_. — Where earlier model generations needed more rules and examples to stay on track, Anthropic found its Fable 5 class actually performs worse with too much steering — it cut 80 percent of Claude Code's system prompt after realising the model is more imaginative than the examples it was given.
- 📰 **[Mark Zuckerberg tells staff that AI agents haven't progressed as quickly as he'd hoped](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/mark-zuckerberg-tells-staff-that-ai-agents-havent-progressed-as-quickly-as-hed-hoped/)** — _TechCrunch AI_. — Despite reassigning 7,000 employees to AI groups and planning $145 billion in infrastructure spend this year, Zuckerberg told staff at an internal town hall that AI agents haven't accelerated as Meta expected — admitting the payoff from its restructuring hasn't come to fruition yet.
- 📰 **[Meta quietly launches vibe-coded gaming app Pocket](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/meta-quietly-launches-vibe-coded-gaming-app-pocket/)** — _TechCrunch AI_. — Meta repurposed the team behind vibe-coded gaming platform Gizmo into its own Pocket app, letting anyone type a prompt and get a shareable mini game — joining a growing lineup of AI creation tools alongside Vibes for AI video and Meta AI for images.
- 🔵 **[New York City educators and industry leaders gathered at Google's offices to shape the future of AI in classrooms.](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/nyc-ai-summit/)** — _Google AI blog_. — Google, the New York Jobs CEO Council, and Urban Assembly convened 150 education and industry leaders for an AI-in-classrooms summit, the throughline being that as AI streamlines workflows, human skills like adaptability and critical judgment matter more, with hands-on sessions on Google AI mode, NotebookLM, and aiEDU's Vibe Coding.
