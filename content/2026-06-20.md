**Reddit — Claude & Coding**

- 📝 **[Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5: Capabilities](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-capabilities)** — _Don't Worry About the Vase (Zvi)_. — Zvi breaks down the system card for both models and the government takedown triggered by Amazon finding a "fix this code" jailbreak — arguing the real lesson is that any usable LLM can be jailbroken, and the export-control approach forces full takedowns of both models, not just Fable.

**Reddit — AI World**

- 📰 **[Norway imposes near ban on AI in elementary school](https://www.reuters.com/technology/norway-imposes-near-ban-ai-elementary-school-2026-06-19/)** — _Hacker News (ai/llm/claude)_. — Norway's PM Jonas Gahr Stoere announced a near-total ban on generative AI for students aged 6-13 starting this August, with supervised use for ages 14-16 and encouraged adoption for 17-19, citing risks of children skipping fundamental learning steps like reading, writing, and mathematics.

- 📝 **[[AINews] GLM > GPT? GLM-5.2 passes vibe check; Z.ai forecasts Open Fable by December](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-glm-gpt-glm-52-passes-vibe)** — _Latent Space (swyx)_. — GLM-5.2 is clearing community "daily driver" bars from multiple practitioners (Jeremy Howard, Mat Velloso), scoring above GPT-5.5 on the new AA-Briefcase knowledge-work benchmark at one-sixth the cost, while Z.ai hints an open Fable-class model by December.

- 🧪 **[Reliability without Validity: A Systematic, Large-Scale Evaluation of LLM-as-a-Judge Models Across Agreement, Consistency, and Bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19544)** — _arXiv cs.CL_. — Across 21 judges, 9 providers, and 541,000 judgments, the study finds universal kappa deflation (a substantial gap between exact-match agreement and Cohen's κ), judge rankings shifting by 14 positions across benchmarks, and a consistency-bias paradox where high test-retest reliability masks severe position bias in two production judges.
