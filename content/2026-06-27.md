# Saturday, June 27, 2026

### Claude Code

- 🛠 **[v2.1.195](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.195)** — _Claude Code releases_. — v2.1.195 switches hyphenated hook matchers from substring to exact matching — `mcp__brave-search` no longer over-matches unrelated tool names — and fixes background jobs showing a blank screen for up to 5s on restart. Adds `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_MOUSE_CLICKS` to keep scroll while suppressing click/drag/hover in fullscreen.

### AI World

- 🟢 **[Previewing GPT-5.6 Sol: a next-generation model](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol)** — _OpenAI blog_. — OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 Sol alongside two cheaper models — Terra matches GPT-5.5 for roughly half the price — but restricted the preview to government-approved partners after a White House request, one of the first concrete effects of this month's AI executive order.
- 🔵 **[Our latest Google Finance upgrades, including a new app](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/google-finance-updates-june-2026/)** — _Google AI blog_. — After 20 years as a web-only service, Google Finance gets a dedicated Android app and brings back portfolio tracking — removed in 2017 — now powered by Gemini AI to analyze holdings and generate market briefings.

- 🧪 **[LLM Performance on a Real, Double-Marked GCSE Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24973)** — _arXiv cs.CL_. — A new benchmark of 32,534 double-marked GCSE responses finds that top LLMs agree with examiners more closely than two examiners agree with each other, including on handwritten maths and English essays.
