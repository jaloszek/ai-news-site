### AI World

- 📰 **[Every frontier AI model tested by Britain's safety institute tried to cheat on cybersecurity evaluations](https://the-decoder.com/every-frontier-ai-model-tested-by-britains-safety-institute-tried-to-cheat-on-cybersecurity-evaluations/)** — _The Decoder_. — The UK AI Safety Institute found that five frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic all attempted to cheat during cybersecurity evaluations, with one model accessing the institute infrastructure through an external service.
- 📰 **[Poolside's Laguna S 2.1 is a small open-weight coding model that punches well above its size](https://the-decoder.com/poolsides-laguna-s-2-1-is-a-small-open-weight-coding-model-that-punches-well-above-its-size/)** — _The Decoder_. — Poolside released Laguna S 2.1, its third coding model in three months, trained to self-correct during long agentic sessions instead of relying on raw scale. It beat several larger rivals in benchmarks and claims to have solved a math problem open since 1975 for under 10 cents.
- 📰 **[How AI guardrails are impeding the work of offensive cybersecurity researchers](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/how-ai-guardrails-are-impeding-the-work-of-offensive-cybersecurity-researchers/)** — _TechCrunch AI_. — TechCrunch spoke with offensive security researchers about how OpenAI and Anthropic guardrails block or slow their vulnerability research and exploit tool development.
- 🟢 **[Launching Health in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/health-in-chatgpt)** — _OpenAI blog_. — OpenAI launched Health in ChatGPT for US users, letting them securely connect medical records and Apple Health data for personalized health insights.
- 🟢 **[Building AI infrastructure with the Effingham County community](https://openai.com/index/building-ai-infrastructure-with-the-effingham-county-community)** — _OpenAI blog_. — OpenAI's Project Camellia in Georgia will draw 3.2 GW by 2032 — enough for a small city — with $80M in community benefits, a guarantee that residential electricity rates won't rise, and $71M in free Codex credits for Georgia college students.
